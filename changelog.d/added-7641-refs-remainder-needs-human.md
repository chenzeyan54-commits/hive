- A merged `Refs #N` PR now carries what it left open back to the issue, and can hand the rest to a human ([#7641](https://github.com/hivecommons/hive/issues/7641)). The task-list sweep from [#7071](https://github.com/hivecommons/hive/issues/7071) only looked at issues with `- [ ]` boxes, so a hive-filed finding written as prose stayed silent after a partial PR merged: nothing on the issue said which PR landed or what remained, and once the 72h merged-claim window expired it was offered as fresh work again — Danathar/sensi#196 sat that way after Danathar/sensi#198 shipped three of its six edits. The sweep now posts one in-place-edited comment on such an issue quoting the PR body's remainder section (`## What remains`, `## What this deliberately leaves undone`, …) or the `Refs` line itself. When that `Refs` line carries the word `needs-human`, the sweep also applies the `needs-human` label and the issue leaves the actionable set — governor counts, kick prompts, plan-from-label and the contribute queue — the way an escalated PR leaves fix dispatch, until a person removes the label or closes the issue. The word is the only signal: prose saying the same thing leaves the issue in the queue. Every PR-body policy template and `src/docs/hive-open-pr.md` document the form.
